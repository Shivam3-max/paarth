## SECTION 3 — THE SEVEN STUDY CLUSTERS

**Headline:** What you study inside Marketing at Paarth

### 1. Consumer Psychology & Positioning
Why people buy, how buying decisions are actually made, and how to occupy a clear position in the customer's mind.
*Topics:* buying behaviour and the five buying roles, changing consumer behaviour, positioning, value proposition, differentiation, messaging clarity.

### 2. Brand Building
How brands accumulate equity — and how consistency at every touchpoint becomes a moat.
*Topics:* brand equity, touchpoint consistency, visual presentation of services, how brands become viral, brand vs performance thinking.

### 3. Content & Social Media
Build content systems that produce attention on schedule, not by accident.
*Topics:* the 10 Cs of content creation, platform strategy (Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn), hooks and retention, short-form vs long-form, content calendars and repurposing systems.

### 4. Performance Marketing & Paid Media
Run campaigns that are measured in customer acquisition cost, not likes.
*Topics:* ad campaign design, the must-haves of an ad, Meta and Google ads, guerrilla / moment / value-edge marketing, reducing cost of customer acquisition (COCA), budget and bid logic.

### 5. Funnels & Conversion
Turn attention into pipeline, and pipeline into revenue.
*Topics:* the marketing funnel, landing pages, lead generation and lead magnets, lead scoring, WhatsApp and email nurture sequences, conversion rate thinking.

### 6. SEO & Organic Growth
Be found when demand already exists, and build owned audiences that no algorithm can take away.
*Topics:* search fundamentals, keyword and intent research, on-page and content SEO, Google Business Profile and local search, newsletters and owned distribution.

### 7. Personal Branding & Marketing Analytics
The founder is the first distribution channel. Learn to build visibility — and to read the numbers behind every campaign.
*Topics:* personal brand architecture, authority content, case-study storytelling, marketing metrics and dashboards, ROI of marketing spend, pricing as a marketing decision.
