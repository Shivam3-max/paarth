# Paarth — Curriculum Architecture (Master Map)

This document maps the source material (LFP 2.0 frameworks, 10 Day MBA Days 1–9, Business ka Booster) into Paarth's three pillars. It is the internal reference behind the public "What We Teach" pages.

> **IP note:** The source frameworks are Bada Business / Dr. Vivek Bindra proprietary material. On the public site we teach the *underlying concepts* under Paarth's own module names. Branded names (LFP, Navratnas, RiSiMiS, "Business ka Booster", "10 Day MBA") must NOT appear anywhere on the website.

---

## PILLAR 3 — BUSINESS LEADERSHIP (10 capability areas)

The original blueprint had 8 capability areas. The source material adds two more that are too substantial to fold in: **Strategy & Competitive Advantage** and **Business Finance**.

### 1. Entrepreneurial Foundations
From: 10DMBA Day 1 (Solopreneur → Entrepreneur), Booster (Belief System, Build Your Core), LFP 7 Steps to Goals, Personal Leadership, Busyness to Business, See Things Better Than They Are
- Freelancer → solopreneur → entrepreneur transition
- Goal setting and aspiration-vs-action assessment
- Personal leadership and self-management
- Moving from being busy to building a business
- Vision-casting for teams

### 2. Market Research & Product Thinking
From: 10DMBA Day 2, LFP Focus Pilot Market, Product Portfolio, Business Expansion with Non-Consumers, Customer's Money-Making Model
- Buying behaviour: initiator / influencer / decider / buyer / consumer
- Market validation interviews; the prepay/deposit/LOI/pilot "litmus test"
- Primary vs secondary research
- Pilot markets before full launch
- Portfolio thinking; finding non-consumers as expansion
- Understanding how your customer makes money (B2B empathy)

### 3. Strategy & Competitive Advantage
From: 10DMBA Day 3, LFP Critical Success Factors, VRIO, 5P's of Strategy Reformation, Execution Excellence in Strategy, Speed of Implementation, Creating Entry Barriers, Switching Barriers, Change Management, 5R Module, Arrow 1 & 2
- Critical success factors; focus (profitability 1/3rd rule, cycle time, cost reduction)
- VRIO: valuable, rare, inimitable, organized
- Strategy reformation (5Ps) and execution excellence
- Speed of implementation as competitive edge
- Entry barriers and switching barriers (moats)
- Leading change inside an organization

### 4. Communication & Influence
From: 10DMBA Day 5, Booster (MCD Model of Leadership), LFP Personal & Interpersonal Effectiveness
- One-to-many communication; public speaking as a skill, not talent
- Storytelling ("story fusion"), 8 types of hooks, golden statements
- Openings and closings for impression and retention
- Interpersonal effectiveness at work

### 5. Sales & Negotiation
From: 10DMBA Day 6, Booster (Need-Based Selling, Insight & Discovery Questions, Profitable Push Sales, Lead Scoring)
- Need-based selling; need creation
- SPIN, A-R-C, PAS question frameworks; BYAF and soft-close techniques
- Discovery questions; objection handling
- Negotiation fundamentals
- Lead scoring and pipeline discipline

### 6. Customer Experience & Retention
From: LFP Customer Experience 1 & 2, Customer Service, Customer Success, Booster (Customer Touchpoints, Net Promoter Score)
- Touchpoint mapping; consistent experience = brand equity
- Service vs success: reactive vs proactive
- NPS and retention measurement
- Switching barriers as retention design

### 7. Hiring & People Systems
From: 10DMBA Day 7, LFP How To Hire, Building Capable Manpower, Cultivating Navratnas*, Talent Engagement, Training & Evaluation, Culture of High Performance, ESOP, Booster (Evaluate then Hire, Stay Meetings, Incentives, Catch People Doing Right Things)
(*public name: "Growing Your Core Team")
- Talent acquisition for small companies; relevant > merely talented
- Structured evaluation before hiring
- Onboarding, training, and evaluation loops
- Engagement, stay interviews, incentive design, recognition
- High-performance culture; ESOPs as retention and ownership tools

### 8. Productivity & Execution
From: 10DMBA Day 8, LFP Replace Your Self, Speed of Implementation, Effective Planning & Time Management book
- Time analysis, 4D prioritization, Big 5 / Small 5 goals
- Meetings as investment: cost, ROI, agenda discipline
- Fairness > equality; leading vs lagging indicators
- Replacing yourself: delegation ladders (I do → we do → you do)

### 9. Operations, Systems & Scale
From: 10DMBA Day 9, Booster (SOPs & TAT), LFP Expand Your Business, Increase Your Business Turnover, Change Management
- SOP creation (6-step method, RACI), TAT standards
- Job shadowing to convert capability into repeatability
- Dashboards and review rhythms; CEO's office (PA / EA / Chief of Staff)
- Professionalizing family-run businesses; succession planning
- J-curve of expansion; turnover growth levers

### 10. Business Finance
From: LFP Cash Flow Management, Financial Planning, Investment in Working Capital, Credit & Further Borrowing, Inventory Turnover Ratio, Profitability & ROE, Return on Investment, Value at Risk, Passive Income, Product Pricing Strategy, Business Health Checkup, Booster (Debt Restructuring, ROI with Learning Curve)
- Cash flow vs profit; working capital cycles
- Inventory turnover; unit economics
- ROI, ROE, profitability analysis
- Credit, borrowing, and debt restructuring
- Risk (value at risk), financial planning, passive income
- The periodic "business health checkup"

## PILLAR 1 — MARKETING (7 study clusters)

From: 10DMBA Day 4, LFP Brand Equity, Product Positioning & Value Proposition, Product Pricing Strategy, 10C of Content Creation, How to Design Your Ad Campaign, Reduce COCA, Changing Consumer Behaviour, Booster (How Brand Becomes Viral, Must-Haves in Your Ads, Marketing Funnel, Grow Your Turnover, Competitive Collaborative Strategy), Personal Brand dissertations + "all digital marketing" mandate.

1. **Consumer Psychology & Positioning** — buying behaviour, changing consumer behaviour, positioning, value proposition, differentiation
2. **Brand Building** — brand equity, consistent touchpoints, visual presentation, virality mechanics
3. **Content & Social Media** — the 10 Cs of content, content systems, platform strategy, hooks and retention
4. **Performance Marketing** — ad campaign design, ad must-haves, Meta/Google ads, guerrilla / moment / value-edge marketing, reducing customer acquisition cost (COCA)
5. **Funnels & Conversion** — marketing funnel, landing pages, lead generation, lead scoring, WhatsApp/email nurture
6. **SEO & Organic Growth** — search, discoverability, owned audiences (added per "all digital marketing")
7. **Personal Branding & Analytics** — founder brand, distribution advantage, marketing metrics, pricing as marketing

## PILLAR 2 — AI (6 study clusters)

Defined by the blueprint (research/creation/execution/operations) expanded to full curriculum:

1. **AI Foundations & Prompting** — how models work (practically), prompt craft, judgment and verification
2. **AI for Research & Thinking** — market research, synthesis, competitive analysis, decision support
3. **AI for Content & Creative** — writing systems, visual ideation, campaign assets, repurposing engines
4. **AI for Websites & Digital Assets** — AI-assisted site building, landing pages, copy + design workflows
5. **AI Automation & Agents** — workflow automation, agent tools, connecting AI to business processes
6. **AI for Business Operations** — SOPs, documentation, dashboards, reporting, internal knowledge systems
