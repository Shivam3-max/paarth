## SECTION 2 — HOW THE CURRICULUM IS BUILT

**Headline:** Frameworks, not folklore

**Body:** Every topic at Paarth is taught the same way: a named framework, the business logic behind it, a real Indian or global case, and an application exercise. You don't leave with notes. You leave with a system you can run — in your job, your practice, or your company.

**The teaching loop (4 steps):**
1. **Framework** — the model, named and diagrammed
2. **Logic** — why it works: the economics and psychology underneath
3. **Case** — a real business that lived it
4. **Application** — you run it on your own business or work the same week

## SECTION 4 — SAMPLE FRAMEWORKS (proof strip)

**Headline:** A few of the frameworks you will learn to run

- **The Validation Litmus Test** — will the customer prepay, deposit, sign an LOI, or pilot? Demand proof before you build.
- **The Five Buying Roles** — initiator, influencer, decider, buyer, consumer: sell to the right person in the room.
- **VRIO** — is your advantage valuable, rare, inimitable, and organized? If not, it's temporary.
- **SPIN Selling** — situation, problem, implication, need: questions that sell without pushing.
- **The Meeting ROI Rule** — every meeting has a payroll cost; make it return more than it burns.
- **The Six-Step SOP Method** — turn frequent crises into documented, delegated process.
- **The Delegation Ladder** — I do, you see → you do, I see → you do, report → you scale it to your team.
- **The Cash Conversion Lens** — profit is opinion, cash is fact: manage the working-capital cycle first.
- **The 10 Cs of Content** — a checklist that turns content from luck into system.
- **The COCA Reducer** — systematic levers to cut customer acquisition cost without cutting growth.
