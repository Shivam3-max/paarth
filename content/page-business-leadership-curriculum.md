## SECTION 3 — THE TEN CAPABILITY AREAS

**Headline:** What you study inside Business Leadership at Paarth

### 1. Entrepreneurial Foundations
Move from freelancer to solopreneur to entrepreneur. Set goals that survive contact with reality, build personal leadership, and convert busyness into business.
*Frameworks include:* the aspiration–action audit, goal architecture, personal leadership systems, busyness-to-business conversion.

### 2. Market Research & Product Thinking
Understand buying behaviour, validate demand before you build, and think in portfolios instead of single products.
*Frameworks include:* the five buying roles (initiator–influencer–decider–buyer–consumer), the market validation litmus test (prepay → deposit → letter of intent → pilot), pilot-market focus, product portfolio design, non-consumer expansion, the customer's money-making model.

### 3. Strategy & Competitive Advantage
Learn how strategy is actually formed, executed, and defended — and why speed of implementation beats perfection.
*Frameworks include:* critical success factors, the VRIO test for durable advantage, strategy reformation, execution excellence, entry barriers, switching barriers, change management.

### 4. Communication & Influence
Public speaking is a skill, not a talent. Learn to open strong, structure with story, and close so people remember.
*Frameworks include:* story fusion, the eight hook types, golden statements, opening/closing design, interpersonal effectiveness.

### 5. Sales & Negotiation
Selling is need identification plus trust — learn the question frameworks that professionals actually use.
*Frameworks include:* need-based selling, SPIN questioning, ask–recommend–close, problem–agitate–solve, the soft close, insight and discovery questions, lead scoring, negotiation fundamentals.

### 6. Customer Experience & Retention
Growth leaks without retention. Design touchpoints, service standards, and success systems that make customers stay.
*Frameworks include:* touchpoint mapping, service vs success design, Net Promoter Score, retention through switching-barrier design.

### 7. Hiring & People Systems
Wrong hiring is expensive; learn to evaluate before you hire, engage before people leave, and build a core team you can trust.
*Frameworks include:* evaluate-then-hire, relevant-over-talented hiring, structured onboarding and training loops, stay meetings, incentive and recognition design, ESOPs, high-performance culture, growing your core team of dependable operators.

### 8. Productivity & Execution
People do what you inspect, not what you expect. Learn to run your calendar, your meetings, and your priorities like an operator.
*Frameworks include:* time analysis and the 4D prioritization method, meetings-as-investment (cost and ROI of a meeting), leading vs lagging indicators, the replace-yourself delegation ladder (I do → we do → you do).

### 9. Operations, Systems & Scale
Businesses scale on process, not heroics. Learn to document, delegate, and review so quality survives growth.
*Frameworks include:* the six-step SOP method with RACI, turnaround-time standards, job shadowing for repeatability, dashboards and review rhythms, the CEO's office (PA → EA → Chief of Staff), professionalizing family-run business, the J-curve of expansion.

### 10. Business Finance
Most businesses die of cash flow, not competition. Learn the numbers every operator must own.
*Frameworks include:* cash flow management, working capital cycles, inventory turnover, ROI and ROE analysis, pricing strategy, credit and debt restructuring, value at risk, financial planning, the periodic business health checkup.
