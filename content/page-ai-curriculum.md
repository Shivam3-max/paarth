## SECTION 3 — THE SIX STUDY CLUSTERS

**Headline:** What you study inside AI at Paarth

### 1. AI Foundations & Prompting
Understand what models can and cannot do, and how to direct them with precision.
*Topics:* how LLMs work (practically, not mathematically), prompt structure and iteration, context and instructions, verification habits, judgment — knowing when not to trust the output.

### 2. AI for Research & Thinking
Compress days of research into hours without losing depth.
*Topics:* market and competitor research, synthesis and summarization, customer-interview analysis, decision support, structured exploration of unfamiliar domains.

### 3. AI for Content & Creative
Build content engines, not one-off posts.
*Topics:* writing systems for social, email, and long-form; visual ideation and image generation; campaign asset production; repurposing pipelines (one idea → ten formats); brand-voice control.

### 4. AI for Websites & Digital Assets
Ship landing pages, sites, and digital assets without waiting on a big team.
*Topics:* AI-assisted website building, landing page copy and structure, design iteration with AI, lead-capture assets, launch checklists.

### 5. AI Automation & Agents
Connect AI to your workflows so repetitive work runs without you.
*Topics:* workflow automation tools, AI agents for routine tasks, connecting AI to sheets, CRMs, and WhatsApp/email, when to automate vs when not to.

### 6. AI for Business Operations
Use AI to build the systems layer of a business.
*Topics:* SOP drafting and maintenance, documentation and internal knowledge bases, dashboards and reporting workflows, meeting notes to action systems, AI in hiring and training processes.
